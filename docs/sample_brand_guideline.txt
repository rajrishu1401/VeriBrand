BRAND VOICE & MESSAGING GUIDELINES
===================================

Company: TechCorp Solutions
Version: 2.0
Last Updated: January 2026

---

SECTION 1: CORE BRAND PRINCIPLES
---------------------------------

Our brand voice is professional, confident, and transparent. We build trust through honesty and evidence-based claims, not hype.


SECTION 2: PROHIBITED LANGUAGE
-------------------------------

2.1 Absolute Claims
Never use terms that create legal liability or are unprovable:
- "guarantee" or "guaranteed"
- "always" or "never" (in claims about results)
- "100%" (in relation to success, uptime, or outcomes)
- "completely free" or "totally free"

2.2 Hyperbolic Language
Avoid exaggerated terms that undermine credibility:
- "revolutionary"
- "game-changing" 
- "unprecedented"
- "unmatched"
- "unbelievable"
- "miracle"
- "magic"

2.3 Unsupported Superlatives
Any superlative claim MUST be supported by verifiable third-party evidence:
- "best" - requires citation from independent source
- "fastest" - requires benchmark data
- "leading" or "top" - requires market research citation
- "#1" or "number one" - requires verified ranking
- "most" - requires comparative data

Acceptable alternative: "One of the leading solutions" or "Among the fastest platforms"


SECTION 3: REQUIRED DISCLAIMERS
--------------------------------

3.1 Performance Claims
When mentioning "results", "outcomes", "performance", or "success", always include:
"Results may vary based on your specific use case."

3.2 Pricing Language
When using "free", "trial", or "no cost", must include:
"Terms and conditions apply. No credit card required for trial."

3.3 Security Claims
When mentioning "secure", "protected", or "encrypted", must include:
"Security measures cannot eliminate all risks. See our security documentation for details."


SECTION 4: FORMATTING STANDARDS
--------------------------------

4.1 Typography
- Avoid ALL CAPS for emphasis (except in acronyms like API, CRM)
- Maximum one exclamation mark per paragraph
- Use bold or italics for emphasis, not excessive punctuation

4.2 Punctuation
- No multiple exclamation marks (!!!)
- No multiple question marks (???)
- Professional tone throughout


SECTION 5: TONE GUIDELINES
---------------------------

5.1 Voice Characteristics
- Professional but approachable
- Confident without being arrogant
- Helpful without being condescending
- Specific with data, not vague promises

5.2 Acceptable Tone Examples
✓ "Our platform helps businesses improve campaign ROI by an average of 23% (based on 2024 customer survey)."
✓ "Join over 5,000 companies using TechCorp to streamline their workflows."
✓ "Try our 14-day trial to see if TechCorp fits your needs. Results may vary."

5.3 Unacceptable Tone Examples
✗ "The BEST solution you'll EVER need! GUARANTEED results!"
✗ "Revolutionary technology that will change EVERYTHING!"
✗ "Absolutely FREE forever with no catches!"


SECTION 6: EVIDENCE-BASED MESSAGING
------------------------------------

6.1 Citation Requirements
All claims about our product must be:
- Verifiable through internal data
- Supported by customer testimonials
- Backed by third-party reviews
- Based on documented benchmarks

6.2 Data Presentation
When sharing statistics:
- Include source and date: "(Customer survey, December 2024)"
- Specify sample size: "Based on 1,200 responses"
- Note methodology: "Self-reported by customers"


SECTION 7: COMPETITIVE POSITIONING
-----------------------------------

7.1 Comparisons
When comparing to competitors:
- Never name competitors directly in marketing copy
- Use "other solutions" or "traditional approaches"
- Back comparisons with objective data
- Avoid absolute statements like "better than everyone"

7.2 Acceptable Comparison Language
✓ "Unlike many traditional tools, TechCorp offers real-time sync."
✓ "Our pricing is transparent compared to industry averages."

7.3 Unacceptable Comparison Language
✗ "We're better than [Competitor Name]"
✗ "Only TechCorp can do this"


SECTION 8: CUSTOMER SUCCESS STORIES
------------------------------------

8.1 Testimonial Requirements
All customer quotes must:
- Be authentic and verifiable
- Include customer name and company (with permission)
- Disclose if customer received compensation
- Include disclaimer: "Results may vary"

8.2 Case Study Standards
Published case studies must include:
- Specific metrics and timeframes
- Implementation context
- Customer challenges and solutions
- Honest discussion of limitations


SECTION 9: SOCIAL MEDIA GUIDELINES
-----------------------------------

9.1 Platform-Specific Tone
- LinkedIn: Professional, insight-driven
- Twitter: Concise, helpful, engaging
- Instagram: Visual storytelling, authentic

9.2 Engagement Rules
- Respond to criticism professionally
- Never engage in arguments
- Acknowledge mistakes transparently
- Direct support requests to proper channels


SECTION 10: LEGAL COMPLIANCE
-----------------------------

10.1 Regulatory Language
All marketing materials must comply with:
- FTC truth in advertising standards
- GDPR privacy requirements
- Industry-specific regulations

10.2 Privacy Claims
When mentioning data handling:
- Link to Privacy Policy
- Be specific about what data is collected
- Explain how data is used
- Never claim "we don't collect any data" (too absolute)


SECTION 11: REVIEW PROCESS
---------------------------

11.1 Pre-Publication Checklist
Before publishing any marketing content, verify:
☐ No prohibited language used
☐ All required disclaimers included
☐ Claims backed by evidence
☐ Tone aligns with brand voice
☐ Formatting follows standards
☐ Legal review for high-visibility content

11.2 Audit Frequency
- Quarterly review of all published content
- Immediate review if guidelines updated
- Spot checks on social media posts


SECTION 12: VISUAL BRAND & ASSET GUIDELINES
-------------------------------------------

12.1 Approved Brand Color Palette
All visual marketing assets, banners, and ad creatives must adhere to the official palette:
- Primary Brand Indigo: #4F46E5 (rgb: 79, 70, 229)
- Deep Navy: #1E1B4B (rgb: 30, 27, 75)
- Accent Violet: #7C3AED (rgb: 124, 58, 237)
- Slate Dark: #1E293B (rgb: 30, 41, 59)
- Surface Light: #F8FAFC (rgb: 248, 250, 252)

Prohibited background colors:
- Fluorescent Neons (e.g., #00FF00, #FF00FF)
- Distracting saturated red (#FF0000) for non-error marketing banners

12.2 Accessibility & WCAG Contrast Standards
- Any text overlaid on image backgrounds MUST meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards:
  - Normal body text: minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1
  - Large / headline text (18pt+ or bold): minimum contrast ratio of 3.0:1
- Low-contrast text overlay (e.g., light gray on white, or yellow on white) is strictly prohibited.

12.3 Logo Integrity & Sizing
- Official brand mark must be unobstructed with clear space on all sides.
- Do not invert, distort, rotate, or alter official brand logo proportions.
- Brand assets must maintain high visual resolution with no pixelation.


APPENDIX A: QUICK REFERENCE
----------------------------

NEVER USE:
× guarantee, guaranteed
× always, never (in claims)
× best, #1 (without citation)
× revolutionary, game-changing
× 100%, completely free
× ALL CAPS (except acronyms)
× Multiple exclamation marks!!!

ALWAYS INCLUDE:
✓ "Results may vary" (with performance claims)
✓ "Terms apply" (with pricing)
✓ Citations for statistics
✓ Sources for testimonials
✓ Professional, measured tone
